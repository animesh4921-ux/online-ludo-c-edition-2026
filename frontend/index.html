How to play

  • Each colour has two tokens.
  • Any dice number from 1–6 can bring a token out of its yard.
  • Tokens move the full dice value on their first move.
  • Capture opponents on non-safe squares.
  • A six or capture grants another roll.
  • Exact rolls are required to finish.
